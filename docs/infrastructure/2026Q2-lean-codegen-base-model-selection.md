## Executive Summary

For an LMS collective that formalizes **Lean 4** *inference-first* on a single 4×H100 NVL
box (376 GB), the verified evidence points to a **heterogeneous, MoE-heavy lineup** — no
single model wins all four roles. The decisive design fact, independently confirmed
(arXiv:2606.05632), is that **the Lean compiler-as-oracle makes pass@k / refine@k the
right axis, not single-shot accuracy** — which favors *cheap-to-serve* models you can run
in many parallel attempts over one expensive single-shot model.

- **Prover (accuracy ceiling):** **DeepSeek-Prover-V2** — downloadable 7B (32K ctx) and
  671B variants; 671B reports **88.9 % miniF2F-test** and 49/658 PutnamBench, **but at
  pass@8192**, not single-shot — impractical to run at that budget across 50 agents.
- **Prover (oracle-search fit):** **BFS-Prover-V1-7B** (72.95 % miniF2F via tactic-level
  best-first tree search) — architecturally matched to a perfect-oracle/pass@k collective:
  it *scales search over a small model* instead of betting on single-shot accuracy. A
  **BFS-Prover-V2-32B** successor exists (fetched, not claim-verified here).
- **Prover (cost-efficient workhorse):** **gpt-oss-120b** — Apache-2.0, 117B total /
  **5.1B active** MoE, MXFP4 (~60 GB, fits one 80 GB GPU); a 2026 cost-efficiency leader at
  **<\$0.01 per correct proof**, mid-pack on raw accuracy.
- **Planner / autoformalizer / reviewer (long-context generalist):** **Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B**
  — 30.5B / **3.3B active**, GQA (32:4), **262 K native context** (→1 M via YaRN). Cheapest
  to serve at scale → the most concurrent long-context agents per GB.
- **Soft-prompt-genome base:** requirement **met** — vLLM exposes `prompt_embeds`
  (its name for `inputs_embeds`) via `--enable-prompt-embeds` in **both** Completions and
  Chat Completions APIs (as of v0.23.0, 2026-06-15); SGLang has a live `input_embeds`
  parameter. Either MoE above can serve as the frozen base.

**Biggest evidence gaps (do not paper over):** (1) **autoformalization** (NL→Lean
*statements*) has **no** measured model — every survived benchmark is proof generation;
(2) the 2026 entrants the question asked about (Kimina-Prover, Goedel-Prover-V2,
DeepSeek-V3.2, Kimi K2.6, GLM-4.7) produced **no surviving claims** — absence of evidence,
not evidence of absence; (3) exact KV-cache capacity math was not claim-verified.

## Finding 1 — Prover accuracy ceiling: DeepSeek-Prover-V2 (but mind the sampling budget)

**Confidence: HIGH** — official GitHub + arXiv + live HF weights, 3-0.

DeepSeek-Prover-V2 ships **downloadable** 7B (on Prover-V1.5-Base, **32 K context**) and
671B (on DeepSeek-V3-Base, MLA) variants. The 671B "achieves state-of-the-art… reaching
**88.9 % pass ratio on MiniF2F-test** and solving **49/658 PutnamBench**." **Critical
caveat: the 88.9 % is at pass@8192**, a massive test-time budget — it must **never** be
cited as pass@1, and it materially changes the throughput math for a 50-agent box.
Vendor-reported, not independently reproduced. [S1][S2]

## Finding 2 — Oracle-search fit: BFS-Prover-V1-7B

**Confidence: HIGH** on model/method (3-0); the 72.95 % headline was 2-1.

BFS-Prover-V1-7B (ByteDance Seed + Stanford) scores **72.95 % miniF2F-test** via
**tactic-level (state-tactic pair) best-first tree search** with length normalization and
DPO on **compiler-error-annotated** pairs. This is the search/scaling axis that *directly
matches* a perfect Lean oracle + pass@k collective — you get accuracy from search depth
over a cheap 7B, not from a giant single-shot model. SOTA wording is now **stale** (Feb-2025
release; Kimina, Goedel-V2, DeepSeek-Prover-V2, and **BFS-Prover-V2-32B** exceed it). [S3][S4]

## Finding 3 — Cost-efficient workhorse prover: gpt-oss-120b

**Confidence: HIGH** — independent eval (arXiv:2606.05632) + HF card, 3-0.

In the one **independent** 2026 Lean evaluation, gpt-oss-120b was **among the most
cost-efficient at <\$0.01 per correct proof** with *competitive (mid-pack, not top)*
accuracy, self-hosted on an HPC cluster (matches local-first). Architecture: MoE **117B
total / 5.1B active** (128 experts), **MXFP4** MoE weights ~60 GB → fits one 80 GB GPU,
**unmodified Apache-2.0**. Caveats: the eval covers **proof generation only** (no
autoformalization evidence); top models (Gemini 3.1 Pro 92 % miniF2F, Claude Opus 4.7 86 %
miniCTX) beat it on raw accuracy; it showed **0.0 improvement at refine@32** — "competitive"
means cost-adjusted. [S5][S6]

## Finding 4 — The evaluation axis is pass@k / refine@k, not single-shot (validates the project premise)

**Confidence: HIGH** — direct measurement, 3-0.

arXiv:2606.05632 (Klingner et al., June 2026) measures **pass@k and refine@k** on n=50
miniF2F/miniCTX subsets across frontier *and* open-weight models. Multi-attempt/refinement
beats single-shot: Gemini 3.1 Pro **92 %** miniF2F via refine@32; positive refine@32-over-
pass@32 deltas up to **+28 %**, avg +3.14 %. **This empirically confirms the LMS thesis** —
the compiler oracle makes many cheap attempts beat one expensive shot, so model selection
should optimize **cost-per-attempt × parallelism**, not leaderboard pass@1. [S5]

## Finding 5 — Cheapest generalist to serve at scale: Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B

**Confidence: HIGH** — official Qwen cards + config.json, 3-0.

MoE **30.5B total / 3.3B active** (128 experts, 8 active, **48 layers**), **GQA 32 Q / 4 KV
heads** (NOT MLA), **262,144 native context** → 1 M via YaRN. The 3.3B active footprint +
long context make it the best fit for **many concurrent long-context** planner/reviewer/
autoformalizer agents on a shared 376 GB pool. Caveat: GQA 32:4 reduces KV vs MHA but does
**not** compress to a latent like MLA, so per-token KV cost is *higher than a DeepSeek-class
MLA model at equal context*; the 1 M figure is a YaRN extension, not validated accuracy. [S7]

## Finding 6 — Soft-prompt-genome arm: `inputs_embeds` is supported on both serving stacks

**Confidence: HIGH** — vLLM docs/PRs + SGLang issue, 3-0.

- **vLLM**: accepts pre-computed embeddings via `prompt_embeds` (torch tensor
  `(seq_len, hidden_size)`, **explicitly not limited to the token vocabulary**), enabled by
  `--enable-prompt-embeds`, in **both Completions and Chat Completions APIs** (Chat support
  landed v0.23.0, 2026-06-15; the v1-engine port merged Sep 2025). Flag is "trusted users
  only" (wrong-shape tensors can crash the engine). [S8][S9][S10]
- **SGLang**: live `input_embeds` parameter in `GenerateReqInput` accepting nested
  per-token float arrays — the exact shape soft-prompt tuning needs. Wrinkle: API expects
  `input_ids` alongside `input_embeds`. [S11]

This **clears the binding technical risk** for the soft-prompt-genome arm: a frozen
low-active MoE (gpt-oss-120b or Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B) can be served with injected soft
prefixes on either stack.

## 3. 376 GB fit — derived capacity (NOT benchmark-verified)

> KV/token = `2 × n_layers × n_kv_heads × head_dim × bytes`. Architecture facts (layers,
> kv-heads, active params, context) are **verified**; `head_dim=128` and the GB results are
> **my derivation**. FP8-KV-cache as a 2× lever is **unverified** (claims refuted) — treat
> as untested, not a free win.

| Model | Total / active | Attn | Weights (4-bit / FP8) | KV/token (FP16, derived) | Native ctx |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B | 30.5B / 3.3B | GQA 32:4 | ~16 / ~30 GB | ~96 KB | 262 K (→1 M YaRN) |
| gpt-oss-120b | 117B / 5.1B | GQA + sliding-window | MXFP4 ~60 GB | low–moderate† | (card) |
| DeepSeek-Prover-V2-7B | 7B dense | MHA | ~4 / ~7 GB | moderate | 32 K |
| BFS-Prover-V1-7B | 7B dense | MHA | ~4 / ~7 GB | moderate | (search-driven) |
| DeepSeek-Prover-V2-671B | 671B / 37B | MLA | ~335 / ~671 GB | ~70 KB | 128 K |

†gpt-oss alternates sliding-window/dense attention with sinks, so effective KV is below a
full-context GQA model; exact bytes not verified.

**Reading the box.** The 671B prover **alone** nearly fills 376 GB at 4-bit (~335 GB → ~40 GB
KV left) and is only sane at low-k or as a swap-in escape hatch — **not** a 50-agent
workhorse, especially given its 88.9 % is a pass@8192 figure. The practical always-on
lineup is **light**: Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B (~30 GB) + gpt-oss-120b (~60 GB) + DeepSeek-Prover-V2-7B
(~7 GB) + BFS-Prover-7B (~7 GB) ≈ **~105 GB weights, leaving ~250+ GB for KV** across all
concurrent agents. Using Qwen3-Coder's derived 96 KB/token: ~250 GB / 96 KB ≈ **~2.7 M live
tokens** total → e.g. **50 agents @ ~50 K context**, or fewer agents at the full 262 K.

## Caveats & time-sensitivity

- **Vendor-reported scores.** Only the cross-model eval (arXiv:2606.05632) is independent,
  and it uses n=50 subsets, proof-generation only. 88.9 % / 72.95 % are author self-reports.
- **Sampling budget.** DeepSeek-Prover-V2-671B's 88.9 % is **pass@8192** — never cite as
  pass@1; it dominates the cost/throughput math.
- **2026-entrant coverage gap.** Kimina-Prover/-Distill, Goedel-Prover-V2, BFS-Prover-V2-32B,
  DeepSeek-V3.2, Kimi K2.6, GLM-4.7 produced **no surviving claims** — re-research before
  committing. The fast-moving prover leaderboard should be re-checked at decision time.
- **KV capacity math is derived, not measured.** FP8-KV-cache savings figures were
  **refuted** — do not assume a clean 2× context win from `--kv-cache-dtype fp8`.
- **Autoformalization is unevidenced** — provisional assignment only.
